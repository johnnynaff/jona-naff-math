Overview

My research concerns stochastic evolution equations: stochastic differential equations (SDEs) in finite dimension, and parabolic stochastic partial differential equations (SPDEs) such as stochastic heat equations. A typical model is

$$ dX_t = b(X_t)\,dt + dB^H_t, \qquad \partial_t u = \Delta u + b(u) + \dot W^H, $$

where the drift \(b\) may be very irregular — bounded, Hölder continuous, integrable or even a distribution — and the noise \(B^H\), \(W^H\) may be fractional, with Hurst index \(H \in (0,1)\).

Without noise, such equations can have no solution or infinitely many. The noise changes the picture: it can regularize the dynamics and restore existence and uniqueness. I study this mechanism and its consequences on three fronts — the well-posedness theory, the convergence of numerical schemes that approximate the solutions, and the behaviour of solutions and schemes over long times.
