Biography

About

PhD candidate in mathematics working at the interface of stochastic analysis and numerical analysis.

I am a PhD candidate in mathematics at the Laboratoire de Mathématiques et de leurs Applications de Pau (LMAP, UMR CNRS 5142), part of the Université de Pau et des Pays de l’Adour. My thesis is supervised by Charles-Edouard Bréhier (LMAP) and Ludovic Goudenège (LaMME, Université d’Évry Paris-Saclay).

My work sits at the meeting point of stochastic analysis and numerical analysis. I study stochastic differential equations and parabolic stochastic partial differential equations whose coefficients are irregular — sometimes so irregular that the equation without noise is ill-posed — and ask two kinds of questions: when do these equations have a unique, stable solution, and how accurately can that solution be approximated by a numerical scheme, including over long time horizons?

Alongside research, I teach undergraduate mathematics at UPPA — see Teaching.
